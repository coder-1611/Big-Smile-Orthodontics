01 — The first week

What the first week actually feels like.

Tender, not painful · 3–5 days · Then routine

Braces change your day-to-day less than most people expect, and not for long. The first week is the real adjustment period — after that, most Round Rock patients stop noticing them.

Expect tenderness for three to five days after Dr. Asrar places your braces. It isn't sharp pain — it's a dull, bruised ache when you bite down, which is why soft food matters that first week. Adjustment visits, every four to eight weeks, bring a milder version for a day.

Lips and cheeks need a week to toughen up too. The small, smooth 3M Clarity Advanced brackets help, and wax covers anything that still rubs.

02 — Eating with braces

Eat well, protect your brackets.

Hard · Sticky · Chewy · Cut-it-up

Nearly every broken bracket we repair traces back to one of four food groups. Learn those four and you can eat almost normally. Repairs are quick, but they mean an extra trip to Round Rock and can add weeks.

  • Hard — Nuts, ice, hard candy, unpopped popcorn kernels, pretzels. These snap brackets off.
  • Sticky — Caramel, taffy, gum, tootsie rolls, frozen candy bars. They pull wires loose.
  • Chewy — Bagels, tough bread crusts, pizza edges, jerky. The tug-of-war loosens hardware.
  • Cut, don't bite — Apples, corn on the cob, carrots and ribs are fine; slice them off the cob or bone and chew with back teeth.
  • Easy early wins — Yogurt, soups, pasta, eggs, mashed potatoes, bananas, berries, smoothies.

Water also beats sugary drinks, which linger against brackets — see our healthy-smile guide. If the list is a dealbreaker, ask about Invisalign instead.

03 — Cleaning & hygiene

Brushing around brackets and wires.

Brush · Floss · Rinse · Repeat

Brackets add dozens of corners for plaque to hide in, and plaque left against enamel leaves permanent chalky white marks — decalcification. Cleaning is the biggest thing you control here, and it costs five minutes a day.

Technique beats force. Angle the brush down at the top edge of each bracket, then up at the bottom edge so bristles reach under the wire, then trace the gumline.

  • Floss threader or orthodontic floss — A threader carries floss behind the wire; pre-threaded floss is faster. Once a day.
  • Interdental brushes — Cone-shaped “proxy” brushes clean right around each bracket. Keep one in your bag.
  • Water flosser — Ideal for teens who won't floss otherwise. Use it alongside floss, not instead.
  • Rinse after meals — Water clears the worst when you can't brush; a fluoride rinse at bedtime adds protection.
  • Keep your dental cleanings — See your dentist every six months through treatment.

04 — Comfort, sports & music

Small fixes for the sore spots.

Wax · Salt water · Mouthguards

Braces don't hurt; soreness does, and most of it answers to three cheap things. Press a pea-sized piece of orthodontic wax over any bracket that rubs. Swish warm salt water — a teaspoon in a cup — a few times a day to calm irritated tissue and mouth sores. And take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the label for a day after an adjustment.

If your teeth feel loose, nothing has gone wrong: teeth loosen in the bone before they can move, then firm up in their new spot — more in why do my teeth feel loose. A poking wire or popped-off bracket isn't an emergency, but do call — see orthodontic emergencies.

How long does soreness last after getting braces?

Three to five days, worst when biting down and easing by day four. Each adjustment brings a milder version that fades in a day.

What can I eat the first few days?

Yogurt, soups, pasta, eggs, mashed potatoes, smoothies, soft fruit. Cool or lukewarm feels best on tender teeth. Add usual meals back gradually.

Do I really have to give up popcorn and gum?

Yes — kernels and gum are top causes of broken brackets. Sugar-free gum is still risky: it's the stickiness, not the sugar, that pulls wires loose.

Why do my teeth feel loose with braces?

Because they're moving. Teeth loosen slightly in the bone, then firm up once they reach their new position.

How do I floss with braces?

Use a floss threader to pass floss behind the archwire, or pre-threaded orthodontic floss to skip that step. A water flosser helps but won't replace it.

What are the white marks people get after braces?

Decalcification — chalky spots where plaque drew minerals out of the enamel. They're permanent, and preventable with daily brushing, flossing, and rinsing.

Can I play sports or a wind instrument with braces?

Both. For sports, wear an orthodontic mouthguard built to fit over brackets. Most band students adapt in one to three weeks.
