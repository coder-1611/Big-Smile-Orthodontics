Invisalign clear aligners

Virtually invisible · Removable · 12–18 months

Invisalign straightens your teeth with a series of clear, custom-made aligners — no brackets, no wires. Each set is worn for about one to two weeks, gently guiding your teeth into place.

Treatment begins with a digital 3D scan using our iTero scanner — no messy impressions. You'll see a preview of your future smile before starting. Progress visits are roughly every 8 weeks.

Traditional metal braces

The reliable classic · Colorful ties optional

Modern metal braces are smaller, lighter, and more comfortable than ever. They're the most effective option for complex cases — and a great choice for kids and teens who love mixing and matching colorful ties.

Each visit takes just 15–20 minutes every 6–8 weeks. Most patients complete treatment in 18–24 months.

03

Clear ceramic braces

Nearly invisible · Fixed · Comfortable

Not everyone likes the look of traditional metal braces. For some, sporting a silver smile just isn't ideal. However, sometimes there are orthodontic problems that require the power of braces over clear aligners. For individuals who need both the power of traditional braces and a more discreet look, there are ceramic braces as well as 3M Advanced Clarity Ceramic Brackets. These braces have brackets that are colored to blend in with the teeth. While they are not fully invisible, they are much less visible than metal brackets.

Retainers & appliances

Helping you get the most out of your hard-earned results

After completing orthodontic treatment, patients enter the maintenance phase requiring either fixed or removable retainers for a specified duration. For patients with narrow palates, a palatal expander may be necessary before treatment. Dr. Asrar discusses all available oral appliance and retainer options during initial consultations.

We also offer complimentary take-home whitening for Invisalign patients — maintain your results and brighten your smile at the same time.

Life with braces

Seamlessly navigate your life with braces

It's no surprise that getting any type of orthodontic appliance will change your day-to-day life for the length of your treatment. Our team at Big Smile Orthodontics will be here to help you adjust to your oral appliance and make the transition as comfortable and convenient as possible. Below, we have some helpful tips as well as an idea of what you can expect as you wear your braces or aligners. If you have any questions or concerns about life with braces in Round Rock, don't hesitate to give us a call.

  • Brush & floss after every meal when wearing braces
  • Use orthodontic wax for any brackets that rub
  • Wear rubber bands exactly as directed for your best results
