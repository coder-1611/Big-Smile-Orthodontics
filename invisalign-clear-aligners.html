01 — Why patients choose clear aligners

Straight teeth, invisibly.

Virtually invisible · Removable · No diet restrictions

Our Round Rock orthodontic team offers Invisalign clear aligners for patients who need orthodontic treatment but don't like the look of metal braces. Invisalign is a series of clear aligners worn to move teeth — the most aesthetic option, as it's almost invisible, which is why it's such a popular choice among adults seeking orthodontic treatment. It's also easy to keep your teeth clean, far more comfortable, and there are no diet restrictions: you just take them out to eat.

Clear aligners may not seem as powerful as metal braces and wires, but the effectiveness is nearly equal in many cases. With Invisalign, we're able to predictably treat many cases that would have required braces in the past — from slightly crooked teeth to more major dental problems.

02 — How does Invisalign work?

From scan to smile.

Digital scan · Custom trays · Progress checks

Every Invisalign treatment starts with a consultation with Dr. Asrar. She'll examine your mouth, inspect your X-rays, and help determine if you're a good candidate. If you are, our team provides a treatment timeline and cost estimate and takes a digital impression with our iTero scanner — no messy molds. As soon as you decide to move forward, your impression goes to the Invisalign laboratory to craft your first set of trays.

After we teach you how to use and clean your clear aligners, you'll start treatment at home, wearing your trays 20 to 22 hours each day. You'll return every eight or so weeks for a quick progress check — Dr. Asrar makes sure your teeth are adjusting as they should, and you'll receive the next series of aligners until you reach your final result.

04 — Pricing & financing

What Invisalign costs.

$1,000 off · Insurance accepted · CareCredit financing

Having a hard time pinpointing the cost of Invisalign online? The reason is simple: there isn't a one-size-fits-all treatment plan, so there isn't a one-size-fits-all cost. The good news — we provide a price estimate during your free consultation.

What we weigh during your consult:

  • If any preliminary treatments are needed, like gum disease therapy
  • How severe your case of misalignment is
  • If single-arch treatment is possible
  • How many aligners must be made
  • If rubber bands or other orthodontic accessories are necessary

Invisalign vs. mail-in aligners

Almost always, mail-in aligners are cheaper — because they cut corners to cut costs, like bypassing the initial exam and relying on DIY impressions. Some companies don't even have licensed dental professionals overseeing the process, and many patients end up with worsened misalignment. Invest your time and money in a reputable, safe, and trusted orthodontic treatment.

Can you get Invisalign after braces?

It's possible, yes. Many patients who experienced orthodontic relapse in the years following traditional braces achieve a straight smile again with Invisalign in Round Rock. Once we understand your dental history, oral health, and smile goals, we can determine if you're a good candidate for clear aligners.

Can you eat with Invisalign trays in?

Eating with your aligners in can cause them to stain, warp, and even break — remove them first. Rinse them with clean, cool water and place them in their designated case so they don't get thrown away, misplaced, or damaged during your meal.

What should I do if my Invisalign tray broke?

A small hairline crack is safe to keep wearing with caution. If the break is more substantial, call our Round Rock office — we'll determine whether it's safe to move to the next set in the series or better to revert to the previous set while a replacement is made.

Important: do NOT use superglue or any household adhesive to repair a broken aligner.

How do I clean my Invisalign?

Rinse with cool water each time you take them off. Before putting them back in, gently scrub with clear, mild dishwashing liquid and a damp, soft-bristled toothbrush. Use the cleaning crystals from your welcome kit for deeper cleans or extended-wear days.

Will I need to wear a retainer after Invisalign?

The short answer is yes. Consistent retainer wear is what prevents orthodontic relapse — teeth drifting back toward their old positions — and preserves your hard-earned results.

How long does Invisalign take?

Traditional braces typically take 2–3 years; the average Invisalign timeline is only 12–18 months. Minor cases — like a small gap between the front teeth — can finish in as little as 4–8 months.
