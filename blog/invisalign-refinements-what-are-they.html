Fine-Tune Your Smile: How Do Invisalign Refinements Work?

August 29, 2025 · Big Smile Orthodontics

Invisalign refinements may not be an expected step in the teeth-straightening process. However, learning that you need these additional trays to ensure the success of your future smile is worth the additional time and effort. Keep reading to discover how they work and what you can do to stay on track with your treatment.

What Are Invisalign Refinements?

Invisalign clear aligners are crafted so that surprises are less likely to occur throughout treatment. The materials used and precise smile mapping help lower the chances of any unexpected changes that might otherwise happen with alternative orthodontic solutions.

However, when teeth do not move as intended, Invisalign refinements can be the answer. These are additional clear aligners your orthodontist provides to make small adjustments throughout treatment.

The trays look the same but are crafted based on the changes that are necessary to keep your teeth moving in the right direction. Your orthodontist will ensure each aligner is made to address the problem area so that the appropriate amount of pressure is applied.

What Can You Do to Avoid a Delay in Your Treatment?

Since your treatment timeline may be extended as a result of Invisalign refinements, you’ll want to make sure that you continue to follow the instructions provided by your orthodontist to keep everything on track. You can do this by:

  • Wearing your aligners for 20-22 hours each day. This ensures adequate pressure is applied to your teeth so that they continue to move toward their final destination. You will also need to make sure you are switching out your current set for the next one in the series after two weeks (unless otherwise directed).
  • Keeping your regular follow-up appointments with your orthodontist. These quick and easy visits will allow them to check your progress and make sure no additional changes are required.
  • Protecting your aligners by keeping them in a case. Losing or damaging one or both of your trays can cause a setback in your treatment. If you want to avoid a delay, place them in a protective case instead of in a napkin or in your purse. This will keep them safe instead of accidentally being thrown away, sat on, or lost.

Do not be alarmed if you need Invisalign refinements; instead, consider this addition as a necessary component that will make your smile look, feel, and function better than ever in the end.
