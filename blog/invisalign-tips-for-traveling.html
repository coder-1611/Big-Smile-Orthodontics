Jet-Setting with Invisalign: 4 Tips to Stay on Track While Traveling

August 6, 2025 · Big Smile Orthodontics

Wearing Invisalign clear aligners allows you to move through life without the distraction and hassle of metal brackets and wires. Daily tasks are easy to follow when you’re at home, but what about when you’re traveling? Are there additional steps you need to take or considerations you need to make to keep your treatment on track? With the help of these 4 tips, you can continue to watch your smile straighten while enjoying the views on your next trip.

Don’t Stray From Your Routine

Whether you’re at the beach, in the mountains, or sightseeing in a European country, make sure you stick to your oral hygiene routine. It’s easy to get off track when you’re relaxing or spending your time somewhere new, but waking up each day and taking care of your teeth, gums, and aligners is essential. The same is true before going to bed.

Brushing twice daily, flossing at least once, rinsing with an antimicrobial mouthwash, and thoroughly cleaning your trays are the best ways to keep your smile and aligners in the best possible shape.

Wear Your Aligners 20-22 Hours a Day

You may be tempted to take your aligners out more often when the sight of ice cream or a popsicle comes within eyesight while at the beach. While taking them out briefly to enjoy a quick snack is not an issue, frequent snacking is, and it can slow your progress.

Wearing Invisalign 20-22 hours a day ensures your teeth continue to move accordingly. When you forget to put them back in or choose not to wear them, you essentially increase the amount of time you will spend in treatment.

Pack the Essentials for a Healthy Smile

When preparing for your trip, make sure that you pack all the necessities to keep your mouth and aligners clean. These include a travel toothbrush, dental floss, mouthwash, and extra aligners. You might also make sure that you carry some type of over-the-counter pain reliever in case you need to swap out your trays during your trip.

Keeping these items in your carry-on will ensure you have access to them, so you never have to worry about waiting to take care of your smile.

Carry a Protective Case with You All the Time

No matter where you are vacationing this time of year, make sure that you keep a protective case nearby at all times. Since you must remove your Invisalign aligners when preparing to eat or drink, keeping them in this hard case will ensure their safety and whereabouts.

It’s too easy to accidentally throw them away if they’re wrapped up in a napkin, so don’t let yourself become worried about your clear braces. Instead, know they’re safe and tucked away while you enjoy your delicious meal.

Traveling with Invisalign can be just as easy as when you’re wearing your aligners at home. By following these helpful tips, your smile can continue to move while you enjoy your trip.
