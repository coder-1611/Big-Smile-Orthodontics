Keepin’ it Straight: 3 Reasons to Wear Your Retainer

July 7, 2026 · Big Smile Orthodontics

After months or even years of wearing braces or clear aligners, finally completing orthodontic treatment is an exciting milestone. But your journey to a healthy, straight smile isn’t over just yet. Wearing your retainer is one of the most important steps in protecting the results you’ve worked so hard to achieve. If you’re tempted to skip wearing your retainer, here are 3 reasons why it’s worth making it part of your daily routine.

1. It Helps Keep Your Teeth Straight

Your teeth naturally want to shift over time. Even after orthodontic treatment has moved them into their ideal positions, the surrounding bone and soft tissues need time to stabilize. Without a retainer, your teeth can gradually drift back toward their original positions, a process known as orthodontic relapse.

This movement can happen slowly enough that you don’t notice it until your smile looks noticeably different or your retainer no longer fits properly.

By wearing your retainer as directed by your dentist or orthodontist, you help maintain the beautiful alignment you’ve already achieved and reduce the likelihood of needing additional orthodontic treatment in the future.

2. It Protects Your Investment

Orthodontic treatment is a significant investment of both time and money. Whether you chose traditional braces or clear aligners, you likely spent months attending appointments and following your treatment plan to create your new smile.

Wearing your retainer is a simple way to protect that investment. Consistently following your provider’s instructions helps preserve your results and prevents unnecessary costs that could arise if your teeth shift enough to require retreatment.

Think of your retainer as the final, yet ongoing step in your orthodontic care.

3. It Supports a Healthy Bite

Retainers do more than keep your teeth looking straight—they also help maintain your bite. When your teeth stay in their proper positions, they come together more evenly when you chew and speak.

If teeth begin to shift, your bite can gradually become misaligned, which may contribute to uneven tooth wear, difficulty cleaning between crowded teeth, or unnecessary stress on certain teeth. Wearing your retainer helps preserve both the appearance and function of your smile.

Over time, your dentist or orthodontist may adjust how often you need to wear your retainer, but following their recommendations is essential for long-term success.

Wearing your retainer may seem like a minor part of your routine, but it has a major impact on maintaining your orthodontic results. By preventing tooth movement, protecting your investment, and preserving a healthy bite, your retainer helps ensure your smile stays beautiful for years to come.
