Before you come in

Finding us, and what to expect.

Where we are

Big Smile Orthodontics is at 1025 Sendero Springs Drive, Suite 110, Round Rock, TX 78681, in the Sendero Springs plaza just off RM-1431. There is free surface parking directly outside the suite, the entrance is at ground level with no stairs, and the office is stroller and wheelchair accessible. If you are using a map app, searching "Big Smile Orthodontics Round Rock" will land you in the right car park rather than at the back of the building.

We are a short drive from most of north-west Williamson County. Patients regularly come to us from Cedar Park and Leander to the west, Georgetown to the north, Hutto and Pflugerville to the east, and North Austin to the south — typically fifteen to twenty-five minutes door to door.

Office hours

We are open Monday 8:00am–6:00pm, Tuesday and Wednesday 8:30am–6:00pm, Thursday 9:00am–5:00pm, and Friday 8:00am–6:00pm, and closed on weekends. Early-morning and late-afternoon slots go first because they work around the school day, so if you need one of those it is worth booking a couple of weeks ahead. We keep same-week openings available for new consultations wherever we can.

Your first visit

The first appointment takes about 30 to 45 minutes and costs nothing. Dr. Asrar examines your teeth and bite, takes any X-rays that are needed, and captures a 3D digital scan with our iTero scanner — no putty impressions. You then sit down together and go through what she found, which treatment options make sense, roughly how long each would take, and what it would cost, including what your insurance is expected to cover. You leave with that in writing. Nobody will ask you to decide on the spot.

To make the visit as useful as possible, bring a photo ID, your dental insurance card, and a list of any medications. If another dentist has taken X-rays of your teeth in the last six months, having those sent over can save us repeating them. Completing your new-patient forms online beforehand means you spend your time with the doctor rather than a clipboard. Parents of children under 18 should plan to come in with them, since we will need a guardian for consent and for the conversation about treatment.

Already a patient?

For routine adjustments, rescheduling, or a question about your treatment, calling (512) 828-7900 is almost always faster than email. If a bracket has come loose, a wire is poking, or an aligner no longer fits, read what to do about orthodontic emergencies first — most of these are manageable at home until we can see you, and the page will tell you which ones are not. For a genuine medical emergency, facial trauma, or difficulty breathing or swallowing, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Insurance and payment

We are in-network with most major carriers and will verify exactly what your orthodontic benefit is worth before your first visit — just call with your carrier and member ID. If we are out-of-network with your plan we still submit the claims for you. See insurance and financing for how orthodontic coverage works, our in-house $0-down payment plans, and the current new-patient offer.
